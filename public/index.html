How it works

Four steps. Every time.

  1. 01

    Create your avatar

    Give it a name, choose its appearance and describe its personality. Upload references if you already have a style in mind.

  2. 02

    Create content

    Ask for what you need: a photo shoot, a short video or a voiceover. The studio uses the avatar's identity to keep everything consistent.

  3. 03

    Review and organize

    Compare versions, approve the best and save them in the avatar's library.

  4. 04

    Publish and manage

    Schedule posts, respond to your audience and review results and costs by avatar or project.

The avatar at the center

An identity that stays consistent across every piece.

Each avatar keeps three things that make it recognizable:

  • Identity. Appearance, personality and voice.
  • Memory. Its preferences and your decisions, retained between sessions.
  • Knowledge. Guides, references and sources it uses to work.

It also has its own assistant. Ask it in natural language to prepare a brief, suggest ideas or plan its next session.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

What is an AI avatar in Colblan?

A virtual character whose appearance, personality and voice you define. Colblan uses them as references to keep its content consistent.

Do I need to know AI or technical tools?

No. Describe what you want in natural language and choose from the options the studio generates. The technical details are handled for you.

Can I have multiple avatars?

Yes. Group them by project. Each keeps its own identity, library and accounts.

Can I create content without an avatar?

Yes. A project can be used just to generate images or videos from a brief and references.

Where is content published?

On the accounts you connect to the project. You can publish manually or on a schedule, and review content before it goes out.
